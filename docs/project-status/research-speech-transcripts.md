## New to this theme?

Speech evidence can resolve a visual ambiguity, but fluent text can
still be wrong, incomplete, translated from the wrong source, or
supplied with a misleading provenance label. A transcript comparison is
interpretable only when it holds frames, model, prompt, and decoding
fixed and records how each text was produced.

For example, a question asking “what did the speaker say?” needs
**lexical speech**—the words—not merely frames or tone of voice. **ASR**
converts audio to timestamped text, but a plausible sentence can omit
the needed phrase. An automatic transcript, uploader caption, dataset
reference, and independently reviewed human transcript are therefore
different evidence tiers.

## Current working answer

Use quality-gated Whisper large-v3 as a **Routed candidate** for
selected lexical-speech questions, with a matched frame-only control. Do
not treat its acceptance rule as language recognition or fidelity. Keep
the strict independently produced human-transcript tier **Blocked**
until independent transcriber/reviewer records exist.

<div class="theme-decision-strip" aria-label="Transcript decisions">

- **Use now:** quality-accepted Whisper only for declared lexical-speech
  work, with a frame-only control.
- **Do not default:** transcripts on every question or mechanical
  acceptance as proof of fidelity.
- **Still possible:** alternative ASR, grounded spans, dataset-reference
  controls, and a valid human upper bound.
- **Next test:** a distribution-level selective-Whisper route with
  deterministic fallback and an independently frozen
  transcript/grounding comparison.

</div>

## Evidence at a glance

- **Selected diagnostic:** accepted Whisper improved 4/10 to 7/10 on ten
  lexical-speech rows; this is not a prevalence estimate.
- **Failure mechanisms:** text can miss non-lexical events or contain
  the right token without changing the answer.
- **Blocked:** a human-transcript upper bound lacks independent producer
  and reviewer provenance.
- **Condition boundary:** a one-row Seamless result does not reject
  alternative or multilingual ASR families.

## What has been tried

### Transcript provenance tiers

Define the tiers before comparing them:

1.  **automatic ASR output** — text generated by a specified ASR system
    from the frozen media;
2.  **uploader caption** — text supplied by the uploader, with its own
    unknown authoring and alignment properties;
3.  **dataset recording-prompt reference** — a dataset-supplied
    reference for the recording prompt, not evidence of an independent
    transcript review;
4.  **independently produced human transcript** — a transcript with
    independent transcriber and reviewer provenance records.

No tier may be relabeled as another. In particular, a dataset
recording-prompt reference is not a TRECVID human oracle.

### Automatic transcript ablation

- **Reason for testing:** an early transcript changed an answer, but the
  project needed a label-blind matched test of accepted ASR on lexical
  speech.
- **Tested condition and panel:** identical 12-frame Qwen requests with
  and without a quality-accepted Whisper large-v3 transcript on ten
  selected lexical-speech rows; all 20 predictions froze before scoring.
- **Observed result:** manual accuracy changed from `4/10` to `7/10`,
  with three rescues and no observed degradation on that selected
  lexical-speech panel.
- **Current condition-bound status:** **Routed candidate** for
  quality-gated lexical speech; not a global transcript default.
- **What this rules out now:** dismissing quality-accepted ASR as having
  no diagnostic value on the tested selected lexical-speech panel.
- **What it does not rule out:** routing prevalence, global transcript
  value, non-lexical vocalization performance, transcript-fidelity
  failures, or 2026 accuracy.
- **Possible defect, audit gap, or uncertainty:** **Uncertainty**—the
  selected ten-row panel cannot establish prevalence or a global policy.
- **Reconsider when:** a representative label-blind routing panel
  measures accepted and rejected ASR strata, including regressions,
  under one frozen decision rule.

### Matched full-panel frame/audio audit

- **Reason for testing:** determine whether quality-gated Whisper helps
  when it is added to the same 12-frame Qwen3.5-4B request across the
  complete locally available labeled panel, while preserving enough
  visual/audio evidence to inspect failures.
- **Tested condition and panel:** 475 VQA 2025 training rows with local
  media; identical 12 selected frames in a frames-only control and a
  frames-plus- accepted-Whisper-large-v3 treatment. Predictions froze
  remotely before references were joined locally. Six annotation rows
  without local media were excluded; VTT captions/search data were not
  part of this panel.
- **Observed result:** Whisper was accepted on 424/475 rows. Frames-only
  scored `0.092632` exact match, `0.243914` token F1, and `0.152985`
  lexical METEOR; frames plus Whisper scored `0.141053`, `0.304860`, and
  `0.215266`. Paired deltas were `+0.048421` exact match, `+0.060946`
  token F1, and `+0.062282` METEOR, with 27 exact-match wins and 4
  losses.
- **Current condition-bound status:** **Routed candidate**—the
  full-panel result supports using accepted Whisper as additional
  evidence for declared lexical-speech or explanation questions, but not
  as an unconditional answer source. One reviewed row showed a clear
  content regression from unrelated audio; several other exact-match
  losses were semantic or formatting variants.
- **What this rules out now:** the claim that accepted Whisper has no
  useful downstream value at distribution level under this matched
  panel.
- **What it does not rule out:** a better router, event-aware frame
  sampling, stronger answerer, transcript fidelity, or benefit on VQA
  2026/test data.
- **Possible defect, audit gap, or uncertainty:** **Audit gap**—all 435
  rows with an exact-match error are indexed, but manual
  visual/transcript review covered 48 rows (all 31 transition rows plus
  17 both-wrong examples). Raw audio playback was unavailable in the
  runtime; the audio-side review used timestamped Whisper text and
  source-media provenance.
- **Reconsider when:** a routed comparison freezes a deterministic
  question/transcript relevance policy plus a shuffled-transcript
  negative control and meets predeclared non-degradation and paired-gain
  thresholds on this same labeled panel. The next route should fall back
  to frames-only when relevance fails; broader frames or a larger model
  come after this control.

### Multilingual and translation follow-ups

- **Reason for testing:** determine whether the intended
  non-English-to-English ASR route was sufficiently evidenced for
  multilingual claims.
- **Tested condition and panel:** frozen-30 Whisper `task="translate"`
  on 30 requests, plus follow-up research and a proposed
  language-stratified design; there was no human language/transcript
  reference panel.
- **Observed result:** 29 mechanically accepted outputs could be
  attached to matched requests, but the completed mixed panel lacked
  source-language labels and transcript/translation references.
- **Current condition-bound status:** compatibility evidence for the
  route; **not** multilingual robustness or transcript-fidelity
  evidence.
- **What this rules out now:** claiming multilingual robustness or
  translation fidelity from the completed mixed frozen-30 route.
- **What it does not rule out:** benefit for a declared language,
  dialect, or translation condition under human-referenced evaluation.
- **Possible defect, audit gap, or uncertainty:** **Audit gap**—the
  completed panel lacks source-language and independently reviewed
  transcript references.
- **Reconsider when:** a source-language-stratified panel with
  independent transcript and translation references completes under
  matched controls.

### Seamless diagnostic

- **Reason for testing:** countercheck Whisper translation on the
  difficult speech example with a different speech-to-text translation
  family.
- **Tested condition and panel:** one speech example with identical
  frozen 12-frame Qwen evidence and a SeamlessM4T-v2 large transcript.
- **Observed result:** the English output was materially incomplete and
  the downstream answer remained wrong; relative to frames-only, token
  F1 and METEOR decreased.
- **Current condition-bound status:** **Not promoted under the tested
  condition**; a one-example diagnostic, not an ASR accuracy estimate.
- **What this rules out now:** advancing SeamlessM4T-v2 large as the
  selected speech route on that one controlled example.
- **What it does not rule out:** another language, model revision,
  panel, or independently verified translation route.
- **Possible defect, audit gap, or uncertainty:** **Uncertainty**—one
  example cannot estimate alternative-ASR fidelity or downstream value.
- **Reconsider when:** a matched, independently reviewable multilingual
  panel shows faithful evidence and a predeclared downstream
  improvement.

### External dataset-reference research

- **Reason for testing:** specify a provenance-safe control that can
  compare automatic ASR against a dataset recording-prompt reference.
- **Tested condition and panel:** research design for a small external
  four-video, three-condition control; no TRECVID effectiveness result.
- **Observed result:** the design distinguishes frames-only,
  automatic-ASR, and dataset-reference text while freezing predictions
  before labels load.
- **Current condition-bound status:** an **Open hypothesis**; the design
  is not a TRECVID human oracle or an effectiveness result.
- **What this rules out now:** calling the proposed dataset-reference
  control a TRECVID human oracle or reporting it as an effectiveness
  result.
- **What it does not rule out:** a provenance-labeled, small external
  control that compares automatic ASR and dataset reference text.
- **Possible defect, audit gap, or uncertainty:** **Audit gap**—the
  design has not run and the dataset reference is not independent human
  transcription.
- **Reconsider when:** the declared three-condition protocol runs with
  frozen predictions, exact provenance, and no claim beyond its small
  panel.

### Human-oracle tier

- **Reason for testing:** estimate the upper bound from independently
  produced transcript evidence without leaking answer labels.
- **Tested condition and panel:** none yet; independent transcriber and
  reviewer records are absent.
- **Observed result:** no valid human-oracle result exists.
- **Current condition-bound status:** **Blocked** until independent
  provenance records and a label-blind protocol exist.
- **What this rules out now:** treating uploader captions, dataset
  references, or ASR agreement as a strict human-transcript upper bound.
- **What it does not rule out:** a valid independent human-transcript
  upper bound once its named provenance prerequisites exist.
- **Possible defect, audit gap, or uncertainty:** **Audit
  gap**—independent transcriber and reviewer provenance records are
  missing.
- **Reconsider when:** independent transcriber and reviewer records, a
  label-blind protocol, and matched downstream controls are complete.

<details>

<summary>

Method, result, validity risks, and evidence
</summary>

Uploader captions, ASR ensembles, and dataset references cannot
substitute for this tier. A true upper bound needs independent
transcript production and review, immutable provenance, matching
downstream controls, and answer labels loaded only after prediction
freeze. There is no oracle result to compare or cite.
</details>

## Known failures and cases

- [CASE-002 — Non-lexical event missed by transcript
  gate](failure-library.md#case-002) shows that accepted text can be
  irrelevant to a requested vocal sound.
- [CASE-003 — Retrieved token unused by
  answerer](failure-library.md#case-003) shows that a needed word can be
  present while the answer remains wrong.

### What the evidence supports

- On the selected ten lexical-speech rows, accepted Whisper changed
  manual accuracy from 4/10 to 7/10, with three rescues and no observed
  degradation.
- Transcript output can be included in a label-isolated request path
  while a rejected output becomes null.
- Fluent or mechanically accepted translation is not by itself faithful
  audio evidence; the Seamless diagnostic shows this distinction.
- Dataset recording-prompt references can be a separately labeled
  control, not a TRECVID human-oracle result.

### What it does not rule out

- The ten-row ablation does not establish global transcript use, route
  prevalence, multilingual effectiveness, or effects on non-lexical
  sound.
- The multilingual follow-up does not identify source languages or
  translation fidelity in frozen-30.
- The one-row Seamless result does not reject all alternative or
  multilingual ASR systems.
- The blocked human tier does not imply a zero upper bound; it means no
  valid upper-bound evidence exists yet.

### Confirmed defects, audit gaps, and uncertainties

- **Uncertainty:** selected lexical-speech rows are diagnostic rather
  than a prevalence or global-accuracy sample.
- **Uncertainty:** the rate/Latin-script acceptance policy is not a
  language detector, an accuracy measure, or a human transcript review.
- **Audit gap:** no human source-language transcript or faithful
  translation reference is available for the completed multilingual
  route.
- **Blocked prerequisite:** the strict human tier lacks independent
  transcriber/reviewer provenance records; other text tiers must not
  fill that gap by renaming.

## Open hypotheses and ideas

- A prompt requiring timestamps and evidence spans may convert accepted
  lexical ASR into more grounded downstream answers than the current
  prompt.
- A label-blind human transcript can distinguish audio-evidence limits
  from ASR limits, but only if its provenance is independent.
- Alternative/multilingual ASR might help a declared stratum when
  compared on an identical frozen panel with explicit null and rejection
  controls.

## Cheapest valid next experiments

### Distribution-level selective Whisper route

- **Hypothesis:** a question-only lexical-speech router can add accepted
  Whisper evidence where words are required while preserving the
  frame-only baseline on the rest of the 475-row development boundary.
- **Smallest valid panel or work item:** freeze the router over all 475
  local VQA 2025 training rows before loading answers or using baseline
  failures to select cases. Run Whisper large-v3 only on routed rows;
  rejected or failed ASR must deterministically fall back to
  frames-only.
- **Matched controls and integrity safeguards:** generate and cache one
  versioned 12-frame evidence set per media hash, then run matched
  frames-only and accepted-transcript conditions from those same frame
  hashes. Keep the question-only route, ASR acceptance/rejection,
  transcript provenance, and model/prompt/decoding fixed and label-blind
  until every prediction freezes.
- **Decision or promotion rule:** every row must have a route or
  fallback and every routed pair must complete; accepted-ASR rows must
  improve token F1 by at least `0.10` and METEOR by at least `0.05` with
  non-decreasing exact match; the composed 475-row policy must not
  regress aggregate exact match or token F1; changed answers must show
  more rescues than degradations under a declared hard-negative review.
- **Boundary on what a positive result would establish:** a routed
  Whisper policy for this declared development boundary, not a global
  transcript default, ASR fidelity claim, or 2026 competition score.

<summary>

Method, result, validity risks, and evidence

The completed full-panel Qwen3.5-4B run supplies the denominator and

the route estimate.

### Transcript grounding prompt

- **Hypothesis:** accepted Whisper plus a required
  timestamp/evidence-span grounding prompt improves lexical-speech
  answers over accepted Whisper with the current prompt.
- **Smallest valid panel or work item:** the existing accepted
  lexical-speech rows or a newly frozen matched subset, with both
  transcript prompts.
- **Matched controls and integrity safeguards:** same accepted Whisper
  output, frames, model, decoding, and question; retain a
  frame-only/null control; freeze all answers before labels and record
  the cited span.
- **Decision or promotion rule:** advance the grounding prompt only if a
  predeclared paired improvement occurs without degradation versus
  current accepted Whisper and controls.
- **Boundary on what a positive result would establish:** it would
  establish a prompt effect on that accepted-ASR panel, not transcript
  fidelity or global speech routing.

### Label-blind human-transcript upper bound

- **Hypothesis:** independently produced human transcripts reveal the
  maximum downstream value of faithful transcript evidence on a declared
  speech panel.
- **Smallest valid panel or work item:** a small frozen speech-critical
  panel with independent transcriber and reviewer records and explicit
  provenance.
- **Matched controls and integrity safeguards:** label-blind transcript
  work; same frames/model/prompt/decoding for frame-only, ASR, and human
  conditions; immutable provenance; and answer labels loaded only after
  all predictions freeze.
- **Decision or promotion rule:** interpret the human-minus-ASR and
  human-minus-frames deltas only if provenance and independent review
  records are complete for every included row.
- **Boundary on what a positive result would establish:** it would be an
  upper bound for the declared panel, not a TRECVID-wide oracle or a
  substitute for independently verified 2026 evaluation.

### Alternative or multilingual ASR matched panel

- **Hypothesis:** an alternative or multilingual ASR can improve a
  declared speech stratum without hidden changes to the downstream
  visual pathway.
- **Smallest valid panel or work item:** a matched frozen panel with
  declared source-language/speech strata, including explicit no-speech,
  rejection, and null controls.
- **Matched controls and integrity safeguards:** same downstream frames,
  model, prompt, decoding, panel identities, and scoring; record ASR
  model, revision, language behavior, timestamps, acceptance/rejection,
  and nulls.
- **Decision or promotion rule:** advance only if the alternative meets
  its predeclared paired improvement and control non-regression criteria
  against current accepted Whisper.
- **Boundary on what a positive result would establish:** it would
  support the tested ASR on that frozen stratum, not multilingual
  robustness everywhere.
