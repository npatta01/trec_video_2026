## Method summary

This is the primary heterogeneous fusion submission. It ranks a successful
Gemini 3.7 Flash native-audio/video answer first, then fills the remaining
ranks from Qwen3.6 transcript-conditioned answers, Qwen3.6 native-video
answers, and the frozen Qwen3.5 control after normalized deduplication. Gemini
failed or unavailable rows fall back to the frozen Qwen/control candidates;
failed Gemini text is never selected.

No reference answers, evaluation labels, or annotations entered inference or
fusion. This method summary describes construction and does not establish
answer quality; organizer validation is recorded separately in the current
state and upload sections.

## Evalbase copy-paste answers

### Runtag

`g37_q36_fusion`

### What base model(s) did you use?

> Gemini 3.7 Flash and the off-the-shelf Qwen/Qwen3.6-27B multimodal model.
> Gemini processed native video and audio. Qwen3.6 produced one answer from
> native video evidence and another answer conditioned on an automated speech
> transcript. Successful Gemini answers were ranked first, followed by the
> transcript-conditioned Qwen answer, native-video Qwen answer, and frozen
> Qwen3.5 alternatives after deduplication. Failed or unavailable Gemini rows
> used the frozen Qwen/control alternatives.

### Fine-tuned or off-the-shelf?

Select **Off-the-shelf**. No model was fine-tuned.

### What training data did you use?

> Not applicable. We did not train or fine-tune any model. We used released
> model weights and a hosted Gemini API without reference answers, evaluation
> labels, or test annotations.

### How long did development take?

> Approximately two days for the final Qwen3.6, Gemini, rescue, fusion, audit,
> and validation workflow, building on earlier baseline experiments.

### Compute resources

> Qwen3.6-27B inference used four H100 80 GB GPU shards with vLLM. Automated
> speech transcription used a separate GPU task. Gemini 3.7 Flash inference
> used the hosted Google API. The final organizer file contains 2,443 queries
> with 10 answers each and passed the official validator with zero errors and
> zero warnings.
