# TRECVID 2026: next scheduled stage

**Question.** As of 19 August 2026 (America/New_York), when is the next
stage of TREC video being released?

## Answer

“Next stage” is ambiguous. In the 2026 VQA workflow, the next scheduled
release after the completed answer-generation (AG) submission deadline on
17 August is **multiple-choice (MC) testing data on 21 August 2026**. The
next MC submission window opens **11 September 2026** and closes **18
September 2026**. NIST schedules the combined AG and MC evaluation results
to be released back to teams on **28 September 2026**.

Therefore, if “released” means the next data/task stage, the answer is
**21 August 2026 (MC testing data)**. If it means the next official result
release, the answer is **28 September 2026**.

## Evidence and interpretation

The official TRECVID 2026 schedule lists the sequence as: AG testing data
available 17 July; AG submissions open 10 August and close 17 August; MC
testing data available 21 August; MC submissions open 11 September and close
18 September; and AG/MC results released to teams 28 September. The page
labels this a “Tentative 2026 Schedule,” so these are the current posted
milestones rather than a guarantee of an unchanged date.

The task page confirms that AG and MC are the two VQA system tasks and that
they use the same testing videos but have different submission deadlines.
The main TREC site explains the naming change: TRECVID was folded back into
TREC in 2024, while multimedia track guidance remains on the TRECVID site.
Thus “TREC video” could refer either to the VQA task’s next data release or
to a broader TREC milestone; the VQA schedule is the most specific official
source for this project.

## Scope note

The broad TREC 2026 call gives an estimated 30 September date for relevance
judgments and individual evaluation scores and a 30 November–3 December
TREC conference window. The TRECVID-specific schedule instead gives the
VQA result-release date (28 September) and a 16–20 November TREC/TAC/TRECVID
workshop week. Those are different milestones, not necessarily a
contradiction: the former is the broad TREC program schedule and the latter
is the VQA/TRECVID-specific schedule.
