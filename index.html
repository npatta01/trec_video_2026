NP Labs · NIST TRECVID 2026

Answering questions about video.

Given a clip and a question, we generated answers and ranked the choices supplied by NIST. Our runs used Qwen on sampled frames or video, Whisper for speech transcripts, and Gemini on video with audio.

Approach and scores

How the two tasks work

Generate answers

We used Qwen on 12 sampled frames and on video with and without a Whisper transcript. In our Gemini-first run, Gemini answered from video and audio, and Qwen supplied alternatives.

Rank the supplied choices

We matched our saved Gemini-first answer to the supplied choices or asked Gemini to rank them directly from the video.
